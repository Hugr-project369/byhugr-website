Privacy policy

What happens to your words.

Hugr is a companion that meets you as you are. You may tell it things you would not tell anyone else. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens to those words: what we keep, for how long, and what you decide yourself.

0. The waitlist on this website

When you join the waitlist, we keep your email address, the language you read the site in, where you signed up (the website or a printed card), and the date, time and wording of your consent. We use them only to write to you about Hugr, in your language. The legal basis is your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.

The list is stored in a Google Sheet through Google Workspace (Google Ireland Limited), only to keep it for us. We keep your details until you ask to be removed: use the link in any of our emails, or write to privacy@byhugr.io.

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party content.

4. How Hugr remembers you

Hugr's memory has three parts.

  • In your words. This is what you write for Hugr to know about you. You can see it, edit it and remove it at any time under Memory. Each save replaces what was there before. Removing it deletes the text, and Hugr forgets it.
  • Your conversations. You can reread them under History and delete them one by one. When you delete a conversation, its text is removed from the app and from our server, and Hugr also forgets what it learned from that conversation.
  • What Hugr remembers. This is what Hugr learns about you across conversations, in its own words, so it can know you better. You do not see it in the app. It is still your personal data, so we include it when you ask for a copy of your data. Reset memory, deleting your account, or an email to privacy@byhugr.io erases it.

Inside our systems your account and each conversation carry a code instead of your name. That protects you, but the law still counts it as personal data.

5. The AI behind Hugr

Anthropic's Claude model writes Hugr's replies. When you write, your message, the recent part of the conversation and what Hugr needs from your memory are sent to that model to produce a reply. Everything else runs on our own server. You agree to this explicitly when you create your account.

Anthropic processes this data only on our instructions, under its data processing agreement. Anthropic may process data in the United States; that agreement includes the EU standard contractual clauses that protect the transfer. Anthropic's commercial terms do not allow it to train its models on our data.

We do not use your conversations to train an AI model. If we ever want to train our own model, we will decide that separately, update this page, and ask for your explicit consent first.

6. Where your data lives and who else sees it

Hugr runs on Franc's own server in Belgium. Your messages are encrypted on your phone. They pass through a relay server we rent from Hetzner in Helsinki, Finland. The relay cannot open them and keeps no logs; they are opened only on our own server.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can, at any time:

  • see the data we hold about you, including what Hugr remembers;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • get a copy to take elsewhere;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • withdraw your consent.

In the app you can delete conversations under History, and edit or remove In your words or reset memory under Memory. Under Account settings you can switch analytics on or off, withdraw consent and delete your account. For a copy of your data or anything else, write to privacy@byhugr.io. We answer within one month. For a complex request we may need two more months, and we will tell you so within the first month. You receive your data as a file you can take elsewhere.

Withdrawing consent: Hugr cannot work without processing your conversations. If you withdraw consent in Account settings, you can no longer use the app, and we stop processing straight away. You can delete your account on the same screen. If you don't, we erase your conversations, In your words and what Hugr remembers within 30 days. We keep only your login and purchase records, so you can come back to an empty account. Analytics consent is separate: switching it off changes nothing else.

If you think we have mishandled your data, you can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, www.dataprotectionauthority.be) or to the authority in your own country.

8. Deleting your Hugr account

This section is our public page for deleting an account. It applies to the Hugr app, published by Inlight Comm.V., Belgium.

In the app: go to Account settings, then Delete account, and confirm.

Without the app: there is no login on the website. Email privacy@byhugr.io from the address on your account, with the words "Delete my Hugr account". We may ask you to confirm from that address. We act within one month, usually much sooner.

What is deleted: your account and login details, your conversations, In your words, what Hugr remembers, your settings and your analytics data. They disappear from the app at once and are erased from our systems, backups included, within 30 days.

What we keep, and for how long:

  • Purchase records (what you bought, when, and the store reference), for 7 years, because Belgian accounting law requires it. They contain no conversation content.
  • A record that you gave consent (date, wording and version, with a code instead of your name), for 5 years, so we can show we asked.
  • Reports you sent about a reply, without your name, for 12 months after they are closed.

Your subscription: deleting your account does not by itself stop payments to Apple.

  • iPhone (App Store): only you can cancel. Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Hugr and tap Cancel Subscription. The app shows you this link before you delete.
  • Android (Google Play): we cancel your subscription when we delete your account. You can also cancel it yourself in the Play Store: tap your profile, then Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Hugr, Cancel.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. Refunds for the current period follow Apple's or Google's rules.

9. Security

Your messages are encrypted from your phone to our server. Our backups are encrypted. Only the people who need it to run Hugr can reach stored data. Our server logs record when a request came in, never what you wrote. The app locks itself when you have been away for 10 minutes. You open it again with your face, fingerprint or phone passcode.

Hugr is a companion for reflection. It is not a medical, psychological or emergency service, and it cannot call for help for you. If you are in danger, call 112 or your local emergency number. findahelpline.com lists free helplines by country.
